Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

Everything you log is stored only on your iPhone. There's no account, no sign-in and no server — see the Privacy Policy.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Because your data lives on your device, it moves with your iPhone's own transfer: set up the new iPhone from an iCloud Backup, a computer backup, or Apple's Quick Start. With ArcShuttle Pro you can also export a CSV spreadsheet or PDF report at any time (Profile → Export data).

I bought ArcShuttle Pro — how do I get it back on a new or reinstalled app?

Open Profile → ArcShuttle Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription.

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Logging practice and matches, goals, your timeline, profile statistics and reminders are all free, forever. Pro adds trend charts, skill-practice breakdown, streak analysis, your full history (the free version shows the last 60 days) and data export.

My reminders aren't appearing.

Check that reminders are on in Profile → Notification settings, and that notifications are allowed in your iPhone's Settings → ArcShuttle → Notifications. Focus modes can also silence them. Reminders may arrive a few minutes after the chosen time.

Why don't my older milestones appear on the Timeline?

Milestones are recorded from the moment you first open the Timeline, as you reach them — earlier activity isn't added retroactively.

How do I delete my data?

Edit or delete any session, match or goal inside the app. Deleting ArcShuttle from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data from the device.
