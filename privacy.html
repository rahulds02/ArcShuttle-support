Privacy Policy

Short version: everything you log stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, store or sell your data.

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how the ArcShuttle app (“ArcShuttle”, “we”, “us”) handles information. ArcShuttle is a badminton training journal for iPhone.

1. The information you enter stays on your device

Everything you add to ArcShuttle — practice sessions, matches and scores, opponent and partner names, tournaments, goals, notes, your profile (name, playing type, experience level and optional photo) and your reminder settings — is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage.

  • ArcShuttle has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • ArcShuttle has no servers: your entries are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • We cannot see, access or recover your data.

2. Data we collect

None. ArcShuttle contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party data-collection tools. We do not collect device identifiers, location, usage statistics or crash reports.

3. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Photos and Camera — only if you choose to add a profile photo. The photo you pick is copied into ArcShuttle's private storage on your device and is not shared.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on reminders or achievement alerts. These are scheduled on your device; nothing is sent from a server.

You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings → ArcShuttle.

4. Purchases

ArcShuttle Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. ArcShuttle stores on your device only whether Pro is unlocked.

5. Exporting your data

With ArcShuttle Pro you can export your data as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF report. Exports are created on your device and shared only where you choose through the iPhone share sheet (for example email or Files). Once shared, the file is handled by the app or person you sent it to.

6. Backups

Like most apps, ArcShuttle's data can be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), depending on your device settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and by you, not by us.

7. Deleting your data

You can edit or delete any entry or goal inside the app. To remove everything, delete the ArcShuttle app from your iPhone — this permanently erases all of its data from the device (apart from any device backups you keep).

8. Children

ArcShuttle is a general-audience sports app and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If a future version of ArcShuttle ever handled data differently, we would explain that here — and in the App Store's privacy information — before the change takes effect.
